# VULCAN® OMNIPRO™ 220 — QUICK START GUIDE

**Item 57812**

---

## Step 1

**1 — Connect cables as needed for flux wire welding – see back of guide.**

> **[Fig. 1 — exploded diagram] 2 lb Spool Loading.** Vertical stack-up diagram of the spool hub assembly on the welder, showing the order of parts from top to bottom. Data: labeled callouts (top to bottom) — **Wingnut** (butterfly-style nut at top), **Spacer** (short cylindrical sleeve), **Spindle** (post that the spool rides on), **2 lb Wire Spool** (large spool body), **Brake Pad** (disc beneath the spool), and **Welder** (the flat mounting surface/base at the bottom). Caption: "2 lb Spool Loading — Adapter and knob required for 10-12 lb spool."

## Step 2

**2 — Install wire spool. Insert wire through both feed guides and clamp it into feed mechanism. See manual if changing wire size or using solid wire.**

> **[Fig. 2 — diagram] Wire routing into the feed mechanism.** Top-down/side view of the mounted wire spool with the welding wire being drawn off the spool and routed horizontally to the right into the drive assembly. Data: labels — **Wire Spool** (large circular spool at left), **welding wire** (strand leaving the spool), **Feed Mechanism** (drive unit at right, shown with its tension knob on top), and **feed guides** (two inlet/outlet guide points on the drive assembly, indicated by a V-shaped pair of pointers at the feed roller). Arrow shows wire travelling from spool → through both feed guides → clamped in the feed mechanism.

---

> **⚠ WARNING**
>
> To prevent serious injury, read manual warnings and instructions before use.

## Step 3

**3 — Take off nozzle and tip, plug selected cord into welder and into wall, turn welder on, and press cold wire feed switch until wire comes out.**

> **[Fig. 3 — diagram] Nozzle and contact tip removal.** Gun/torch neck shown with its front-end consumables pulled off to the left in an exploded view. Data: labels — **Nozzle** (outer cup, removed first) and **Contact Tip** (inner tip, removed second); a dimension arrow at the gun end of the exploded assembly is marked **2"**, indicating the length of wire to protrude.

> **[Fig. 4 — photo/illustration] Cold wire feed switch.** Close-up of the welder's control area with a hand/finger pressing the cold wire feed switch (white hand icon pointing at the switch). Data: shows the switch being pressed to feed wire out of the gun.

---

> **⚠ WARNING**
>
> To prevent fire and serious injury, keep torch and wire clear of grounded objects while welder plugged in.

## Step 4

**4 — Incrementally tighten wire feed tension knob clockwise until wire will bend from feed tension at 2"-3".**

> **[Fig. 5 — diagram] Feed tension knob adjustment.** Front view of the wire drive/feed mechanism (Vulcan-branded) with a curved arrow over the top-mounted tension knob. Data: arrow labeled **clockwise**, indicating the direction to turn the wire feed tension knob to increase tension.

> **[Fig. 6 — diagram] Feed tension check against a wood block.** Gun/torch held with wire stickout pressed down toward a block of wood on the bench; the wire buckles/bends. Data: the bent wire length is dimensioned **2"-3"**; the block is labeled **wood block**. Correct tension is when the wire bends at that 2"–3" length rather than slipping or stalling.

---

## Step 5

**5 — Unplug welder and replace nozzle and tip.**

> **[Fig. 7 — diagram] Unplug and reassemble.** Two-part illustration. Left: the welder's power plug being withdrawn from a wall receptacle (arrow shows plug pulling away from the outlet). Right: the gun neck with the **contact tip** and **nozzle** being pushed back onto the gun (two arrows pointing left toward the gun, tip installed first, then nozzle).

---

## Step 6

**6 — Hold gun clear of workpiece, then plug in welder, turn it on, and set weld thickness and other settings.**

> **[Fig. 8 — four-panel sequence diagram] Power-up sequence.** Panels left to right joined by yellow arrows. Data: (1) A hand gripping the MIG gun with the torch pointed away/up, captioned **HOLD GUN CLEAR**; (2) the power plug being inserted into the wall receptacle; (3) a hand flipping the welder's power switch ON (white hand icon on the switch on the welder front panel, near the negative "−" and positive "+" output terminals); (4) front view of the OmniPro 220 control panel with both hands on the two large control knobs (left and right) beneath the dual digital displays, adjusting weld thickness and other settings. Orange triangle/pointer markers indicate the knobs and center selector to be adjusted.

---

**BASIC WELDING INSTRUCTIONS AND WELDING TIPS ARE IN MANUAL.**
