# Stick Setup

## Connect Cables

> **[Stick cable connection diagram — diagram]** Three-quarter view of the OmniPro 220 welder with its stick welding cables attached to the front panel sockets, plus the ground clamp and electrode holder laid out in front. Data: Callout "Ground Clamp Cable in Negative Socket" points to the upper-left front panel quick-connect socket (negative, −); callout "Electrode Holder Cable in Positive Socket" points to the adjacent lower/right front panel quick-connect socket (positive, +). Callout "Ground Clamp" identifies the spring clamp at the end of the cable running from the negative socket; callout "Electrode Holder" identifies the stinger/electrode holder at the end of the cable running from the positive socket. This is DC electrode positive (DCEP) polarity for stick welding.

1. Plug Ground Clamp Cable into Negative Socket. Twist clockwise all the way to lock in place.

2. Plug Electrode Holder Cable into Positive Socket. Twist clockwise all the way to lock in place.

## Connect Power Cord

Plug either 120VAC or 240VAC Power Cord into Power Input Socket.

**Note:** Plug will only fit one way.

**WARNING!  TO PREVENT SERIOUS INJURY FROM ACCIDENTAL OPERATION:** Do not plug cord into wall outlet at this time.

> **[Rear panel power input — diagram]** Rear view of the welder showing the black rear panel with a round twist-lock receptacle and, below it, the cooling fan behind a grille. Data: Callout "Power Input" points to the round receptacle (twist-lock style with three angled slots) on the upper left of the rear panel; a small indicator/label sits to its right, and a specification/rating label plate is mounted on the right side of the rear panel. Additional small round connector and labeled controls appear at the lower left of the rear panel.
