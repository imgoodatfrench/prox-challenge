**[Side tabs: SAFETY | CONTROLS | WIRE (active) | TIG / STICK | WELDING TIPS | MAINTENANCE]**

> **⚠ DANGER**
>
> **PARTS MAY BE AT WELDING VOLTAGE**
> **TO PREVENT ELECTRIC SHOCK AND DEATH:**
>
> 1. Keep hands away from Wire Feed mechanism.
> 2. Close door before plugging in, unless using Cold Wire Feed to feed wire through to gun.
> 3. Do not touch Trigger while feeding wire through to gun.

> **[Danger pictogram — diagram]** Safety icon at left of the DANGER block showing a human figure being struck by a lightning bolt from an overhead electrode/energized part, indicating electric shock hazard. Data: no text in icon.

24. Plug either 120VAC or 240VAC Power Cord into Power Input Socket.

**Note:** Plug will only fit one way.

> **[Rear view of welder — diagram]** Line drawing of the back of the OmniPro 220 welder with a leader line labeled **"Power Input"** pointing to the round, keyed multi-pin socket on the upper-left of the rear panel (to the left of the rating/specification label plate). Data: Power Input socket = round keyed receptacle on rear panel; two smaller connectors sit below it; a large circular cooling fan grille occupies the lower half of the rear panel; carry handle across the top.

25. Do not touch the Gun's Trigger. Plug the Power Cord into a properly grounded, GFCI protected 120VAC (20 amp rated) or 240VAC receptacle that matches the plug and turn the Power Switch ON. The circuit must be equipped with delayed action-type circuit breaker or fuses.

> **[Power cord / Power Switch — diagram]** Left: a three-prong power cord plug with a curved arrow showing it being inserted into a grounded GFCI wall receptacle. Right: front-panel detail of the welder with a leader line labeled **"Power Switch"** pointing to the rocker/rocker-style switch located on the left side of the front panel, below the two knobs at the top and above the front output connectors; the "VULCAN" brand name appears on the front panel. Data: switch shown being turned ON; receptacle must be grounded and GFCI protected.

26. Point the Gun away from all objects. Press and hold the Cold Wire Feed Switch until the wire feeds through two inches.

    **The wire liner may come out with the welding wire. This is normal, just push the wire liner back into the Gun.**

    If the wire does not feed properly and the Spool is stationary, turn OFF and unplug the Welder and slightly tighten the Feed Tensioner clockwise before retrying.

> **[Cold Wire Feed Switch and gun wire stick-out — diagram]** Left image: cutaway/interior view of the welder's wire-feed compartment (door open) showing the spool hub knob (large knurled knob, lower left), the drive roll / wire feed mechanism assembly, and a leader line labeled **"Cold Wire Feed Switch"** pointing to a small rectangular push switch mounted on the vertical divider panel above and behind the feed mechanism; the carry handle is at the top and two panel connectors are at the bottom. Right image: the MIG Gun (labeled **"MIG Gun"**) with a dimension callout of **2"** at the nozzle end showing the length of **"Welding Wire"** protruding past the contact tip/nozzle. Data: feed wire until 2 in. of welding wire extends from the gun; Cold Wire Feed Switch is inside the wire compartment.
