17. <u>**DCEP**</u> **Direct Current Electrode Positive Wire Setup for Solid Core (gas shielded) welding:**

> **[DCEP Solid Core (Gas Shielded) Polarity Setup — diagram]** Line-art view of the front/right side of the Vulcan welder with the MIG gun, ground clamp and cables connected. Data: Callout "Ground Clamp Cable in Negative Socket" points with a leader line to the upper/left of the two front twist-lock sockets, where the cable running to the ground clamp (shown at lower left with its spring-loaded jaws) is plugged in. Callout "Wire Feed Power Cable in Positive Socket" points to the lower/right front socket, where the cable from the wire-feed/MIG gun assembly is plugged in. The MIG gun with gooseneck and nozzle is shown at left with its cable bundle routed to the machine. The welder front panel shows the display window and control knobs above the sockets, with louvered vents on the right side of the case.

a. Plug Ground Clamp Cable into Negative (**–**) Socket. Plug Wire Feed Power Cable into Positive (**+**) Socket. Twist cables clockwise all the way to lock in place.

b. Determine which type of shielding gas would be appropriate for the welding you will do. Refer to the Settings Chart on the inside of the Welder door.

c. With assistance, set the cylinder (not included) onto a cabinet or cart near the Welder and secure the cylinder in place with two straps (not included) to prevent tipping.

> **[Step c — diagram]** Illustration of an upright gas cylinder with its protective cap/valve on top, secured by two horizontal straps. Data: Labeled "c"; white curved arrows at each strap show the straps being wrapped around and cinched tight to restrain the cylinder from tipping.

d. Remove the cylinder's cap. Stand to the side of the valve opening, then open the valve briefly to blow dust and dirt from the valve opening. Close the cylinder valve.

> **[Step d — diagram]** Close-up of the top of the cylinder with the cap removed, showing the valve handwheel with rotation arrows and a burst of debris blowing out of the valve outlet. Data: Labeled "d"; text callout reads "Briefly open valve to clean, then close valve." A large black arrow leads from the step-c cylinder down to this cylinder, indicating sequence.

e. Locate the Regulator (included) and close its valve until it is loose, then thread Regulator onto cylinder and wrench-tighten connection.

**<u>Note:</u>** When using C100 shielding gas, connect the enclosed CGA 580/320 adapter to the inlet connection of the Regulator and wrench-tighten. Thread the adapter onto the gas cylinder and wrench-tighten.

> **[Step e — diagram]** Illustration of the two-gauge regulator being threaded onto the cylinder valve outlet. Data: Labeled "e"; an arrow at the cylinder valve/regulator inlet shows the regulator turning onto the valve's threaded outlet; the regulator body shows two round gauges (cylinder pressure and flow/delivery) and an adjusting knob.

f. Attach the Gas Hose (included) to the Regulator's outlet and the Welder's gas inlet. Wrench-tighten both connections.

> **[Step f — diagram]** Illustration of the regulator mounted on the cylinder with a gas hose running from the regulator outlet to the rear/side gas inlet of the welder, which is shown as a front view with control panel, settings chart label on its side, and cooling fan grille at the bottom. Data: Labeled "f"; one arrow points to the hose connection at the regulator outlet and a second (downward) arrow points to the hose's connection at the Welder's gas inlet fitting. A large black arrow leads from the step-e cylinder down to this figure, indicating sequence.
