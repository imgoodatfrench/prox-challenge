12. **<u>Feed Roller Instructions:</u>**
 Check that the Feed Roller is correct for the type of wire being used (solid core or flux-cored) and that it is turned to properly match the wire size marked on the Wire Spool:

 a. Unscrew the Feed Roller Knob counterclockwise.

 b. Remove the Feed Roller Knob to expose the Feed Roller.

 c. Flip or replace the Feed Roller as needed and confirm that it is the correct Roller for the type of wire being used and that **the number showing is the same as the wire diameter on the Spool.**

 d. Screw the Feed Roller Knob back into place to secure the Feed Roller.

> **[Step A — diagram]** Top-right line drawing of the wire drive assembly with the Feed Roller Knob installed; a leader line labels "Feed Roller Knob" at the center hex/knob of the drive mechanism, and a curved arrow at the bottom of the housing shows the knob being turned counterclockwise to unscrew it. A large arrow marked "A" points down-right to the next illustration. Data: Knob unscrews counterclockwise; knob is located at the center of the drive roller shaft.

> **[Step B — diagram]** Second drive-assembly drawing with the knob removed, exposing a shaded circular roller labeled "Feed Roller" by a leader line. A large arrow marked "B" points down-left toward the roller groove detail. Data: With the knob off, the feed roller sits exposed on the drive shaft and can be pulled off, flipped, or replaced.

> **[Feed Roller Groove Detail — diagram]** Two side-view cross-sections of feed rollers, each a rectangular roller body with curved arrows pointing to the groove on each end face, showing that each roller is double-sided (flip to change size). Data: Upper roller — "Solid Core V-Groove", left side = 0.030/0.035 groove, right side = 0.025 groove. Lower roller — "Flux-Cored Knurled Groove", left side = 0.045 groove, right side = 0.030/0.035 groove. Solid (gas/MIG) wire uses the smooth V-groove roller; flux-cored wire uses the knurled-groove roller. The size stamped/showing on the outward face must match the wire diameter marked on the spool.

> **[Step C — diagram]** Third drive-assembly drawing showing the feed roller reinstalled in the correct orientation, with a curved arrow along the bottom of the housing. A large arrow marked "C" points down-right from the groove detail to this illustration. Data: Roller is refitted onto the shaft with the correct groove size facing out.

> **[Step D — diagram]** Bottom drawing of the drive assembly with the Feed Roller Knob screwed back on (clockwise) to secure the roller. A large arrow marked "D" points down-left to this final illustration. Data: Reinstall and tighten the Feed Roller Knob to lock the roller in place.
