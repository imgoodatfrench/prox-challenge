6. <u>**10-12 Pound**</u> **Wire Spool Installation:**
   Remove the Wingnut and Spacer. If replacing a Spool, remove the old Spool and all remaining wire from the liners.

7. Place the Spool Adapter over the Spool Spindle and against the Spool Brake Pad as illustrated.

8. Place the new Wire Spool over the Adapter and line up pin on Adapter with hole in Spool. **To prevent wire feed problems, set the Spool so that it will unwind <u>clockwise</u>.**

> **[10-12 lb Spool Loading — exploded diagram]** Vertical exploded assembly showing the order of parts for a 10–12 lb spool on the welder's spool spindle. Data: From top to bottom the callouts are — **Spool Knob** (large knurled threaded knob, topmost), **Wingnut**, **Spacer** (small block), **10-12 lb Wire Spool** (large spool of wire), **Spool Spindle** (threaded stud projecting up from the adapter), **10-12 lb Spool Adapter** (stepped cylindrical adapter, labeled at left), which seats against the **Spool Brake Pad** and the **Welder Wall** (labeled at lower right). Caption beneath: "10-12 lb Spool Loading". Assembly order onto the spindle: adapter against brake pad on welder wall → wire spool over adapter (pin on adapter into hole in spool) → spacer → wingnut → spool knob screwed into the adapter.

9. Replace the Spacer over the Spool Spindle and secure Spool in place with the Wingnut.

**Notice:** If Wire Spool can spin freely, Wingnut is too loose. This will cause the welding wire to unravel and unspool which can cause tangling and feeding problems.

10. Screw the Spool Knob into the Spool Adapter.

> **[Spool unwind direction — line drawing / photo of welder interior]** Side view of the welder with the side door open, showing the spool compartment (round spool knob at center-left) and the wire drive/feed assembly at right. Data: A dashed circular arrow around the installed spool with the label "Wire must unwind in this direction" and an arrow indicating **clockwise** rotation; the wire path arrow points from the spool toward the wire drive feed mechanism on the right. Also visible: carry handle on top, front panel connectors (round sockets) at lower right of the machine body.

11. Turn the Feed Tensioner knob counterclockwise to loosen it enough to pull it down to remove tension. The spring-loaded Idler Arm will move up as shown.

> **[Feed Tensioner / Idler Arm — two-part diagram]** Two views of the wire drive assembly, before and after loosening. Data: Left view labels the **Feed Tensioner** (knurled knob at top of the drive, with a curved arrow showing it being turned counterclockwise/downward) and the **Idler Arm** (the pivoting arm over the drive rollers, marked with a leader line to the center of the drive). A large right-pointing arrow leads to the right view, in which the tensioner has been swung down and the spring-loaded Idler Arm has pivoted **up** (small curved arrow showing upward movement), opening the drive rollers so wire can be threaded.
