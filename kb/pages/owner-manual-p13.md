13. Loosen the Knob on the Wire Feed mechanism, then insert the Gun Cable Connector through the hole on the Welder front and into the socket on the Wire Feed.

> **[Figure A — diagram]** Cutaway/front view of the welder's wire feed compartment showing the MIG gun cable being pushed in (large arrow pointing left, toward the machine) but not seated. Callouts: "Wire Feed Mechanism" (labels the drive assembly at top left), "Knob" (labels the clamping knob on the wire feed where the gun cable connector is secured), and "Gun Cable Connector" (labels the threaded/barrel connector on the end of the gun cable). A dotted outline highlights the gap between the connector and the socket. Caption: "Incorrect—Connector not fully inserted". Data: Gun cable connector passes through the hole in the welder front and plugs into the socket on the wire feed mechanism; the knob must be loosened first. Two panel-mounted receptacles are visible below the wire feed (a round multi-pin socket and a square-flange socket).

14. Ensure that the Gun Cable Connector is fully inserted into the socket on the Wire Feed mechanism as shown, then tighten the Knob securely. If Connector is not fully inserted, the gas connection will leak, preventing shielding gas from reaching the welding arc.

**NOTICE:** To prevent damage, do not overtighten the Knob.

> **[Figure B — diagram]** Same front/cutaway view of the wire feed compartment, now with the gun cable connector pushed fully home (large arrow pointing left into the machine). Callout: "Wire Feed Control Cable" points to the ribbed cable/plug at the right. Dotted outline shows the connector seated flush against the wire feed socket. Caption: "Correct—Connector fully inserted". Data: Correct assembly = no gap between gun cable connector and wire feed socket; the Wire Feed Control Cable enters alongside the gun cable through the welder front.

15. Insert the Wire Feed Control Cable through the hole on the Welder front and connect it to the Wire Feed Control Socket inside the machine, then tighten the lock ring on the Cable plug. Note that the plug on the Cable fits into the Socket in one specific orientation only.

16. <u>**DCEN**</u> **Direct Current Electrode Negative Wire Setup for Flux-Cored (gasless) welding:**
Plug Ground Clamp Cable into Positive (**+**) Socket. Plug Wire Feed Power Cable into Negative (**–**) Socket. Twist cables clockwise all the way to lock in place.

> **[Figure C — diagram]** Three-quarter front view of the OmniPro 220 welder with the MIG gun (left) and ground clamp (bottom left) cables connected to the two front dinse-style output sockets. Title above the drawing: "DCEN Flux-Cored (Gasless) Polarity Setup". Callouts: "Wire Feed Power Cable in Negative Socket" points to the upper cable connection; "Ground Clamp Cable in Positive Socket" points to the lower cable connection. The control panel with display and knobs is visible on the front of the machine. Data: For DCEN flux-cored (gasless) welding — Wire Feed Power Cable → Negative (–) socket; Ground Clamp Cable → Positive (+) socket; both connectors twisted clockwise to lock.
