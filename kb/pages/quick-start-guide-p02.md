# VULCAN OMNIPRO™ 220 — QUICK START GUIDE

**57812**

## STICK CABLE SETUP

1. Connect ground clamp to negative terminal.
2. Connect electrode holder to positive terminal.
3. Leave wire feed power disconnected.

> **[Stick Cable Setup — diagram]** Three-quarter view of the welder's front/right connection panel with the stick cables attached, numbered callouts 1–3 pointing from the labeled list to the machine. Data: Callout 1 = ground clamp (jaw-style clamp on gray cable) plugged into the **negative (–) Dinse terminal**, the upper/left connector on the panel. Callout 2 = electrode holder (red-handled stinger on gray cable) plugged into the **positive (+) Dinse terminal**, the lower/right connector. Callout 3 = the wire feed power lead / gun trigger lead shown **left unconnected** (dangling next to the panel) for stick mode. Orange side panel of the welder is visible at right.

## MIG CABLE SETUP

1. Connect ground clamp to negative terminal.
2. Connect wire feed power to positive terminal.
3. Connect MIG gun to wire feed.
4. Connect wire feed control inside welder.

> **[MIG Cable Setup — diagram]** Same welder connection panel with a MIG gun, ground clamp, and short wire-feed jumper lead attached; callouts 1–4. Data: Callout 1 = ground clamp connected to the **negative (–) terminal**. Callout 2 = short wire feed power jumper lead connected to the **positive (+) terminal** (the jumper loops from the panel into the wire feed block). Callout 3 = MIG gun's cable/gooseneck connector attached to the **wire feed (Euro-style gun connection)** on the machine. Callout 4 = wire feed control/trigger plug connected **inside the welder** (upper connection point on the panel). MIG gun with trigger and brass-tipped conductor tube shown at left.

### FLUX CABLE SETUP

Follow MIG cable setup but connect ground clamp to positive terminal and wire feed power to negative terminal.

> **[Flux Cable Setup — callout box]** Blue text box (no illustration of its own) referring back to the MIG diagram. Data: For flux-cored (gasless) wire, polarity is **reversed** versus MIG — ground clamp goes to the **positive (+) terminal** and the wire feed power lead goes to the **negative (–) terminal**.

## TIG CABLE SETUP

1. Connect ground clamp to positive terminal.
2. Connect TIG torch to negative terminal.
3. Connect gas line to gas regulator.
4. Connect foot pedal inside welder.
5. Leave wire feed power disconnected.

> **[TIG Cable Setup — diagram]** Welder connection panel with a foot pedal (large wedge-shaped pedal at left), TIG torch with purple-banded back cap and ceramic cup, gas hose, and ground clamp; callouts 1–5. Data: Callout 1 = ground clamp lead connected to the **positive (+) terminal** (lower/right connector). Callout 2 = TIG torch power cable connected to the **negative (–) terminal**. Callout 3 = gas line routed from the torch to the **gas regulator / gas connection**. Callout 4 = foot pedal control plug connected **inside the welder** at the upper control receptacle. Callout 5 = wire feed power lead shown coiled and **left disconnected** for TIG mode.
