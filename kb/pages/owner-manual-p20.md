# (continued setup steps)

4. Turn the Power Switch to the OFF position, then plug the Power Cord into a properly grounded, GFCI protected 120VAC (20 amp rated) or 240VAC receptacle that matches the plug. The circuit must be equipped with delayed action-type circuit breaker or fuses.

> **[Power Switch / power cord connection — diagram]** Close-up line drawing of the lower-left front of the VULCAN OmniPro 220 showing the rocker Power Switch, with a leader line labeled "Power Switch" pointing to it. To the right, the machine's molded power cord plug is shown with a curved arrow indicating it being inserted into a wall receptacle. Data: Power Switch is located on the front panel below the "VULCAN" logo and above the round output sockets; plug must match a grounded, GFCI-protected 120VAC (20 A) or 240VAC receptacle.

5. Set MIG Gun down on nonconductive, nonflammable surface away from any grounded objects.

6. Turn the Power Switch ON.

## Settings

> **[Control Panel — diagram]** Front control panel of the VULCAN OmniPro 220. Center is the LCD display, which shows the title "MIG Stainless Steel", a MIG-gun process icon, and a row of small process icons along the bottom of the screen with a small arrow/pointer indicating the currently selected icon. To the left of the screen are the "HOME" button and two small warning/pause symbols; to the right of the screen is the "BACK" button. The "OMNIPRO 220" logo is at the upper right. Below the screen are three knobs with upward-arrow symbols, labeled left to right: **Left Knob**, **Main Control Knob** (larger, center), **Right Knob**. A small up/down symbol appears beside the Left Knob and the Right Knob. Data: HOME button = left of screen, BACK button = right of screen; the three adjustment knobs are Left, Main Control (center, also pressed to select), and Right.

1. Press Home Button on Control Panel.

2. Turn Main Control Knob until desired process appears on LCD display screen.

3. Press Main Control Knob to select process.

**Note:** Press Main Control Knob to go to next screen. Press Back Button to return to the previous screen.

4. Adjust settings for the selected process.

   a. Polarity and Gas Settings:
   - Plug cables in according to screen.
   - Connect gas according to screen.
   - Set SCFH between 20-30.

   > **[Polarity and Gas screen — diagram]** LCD screen graphic showing cable polarity and shielding gas. At the left is a "–" (negative) symbol above a cable plug icon that leads down to a work clamp (ground clamp) icon; at the right is a "+" (positive) symbol above a cable plug icon that leads to the MIG gun/torch icon (drawn as a U-shaped gun connection). Text at the bottom of the screen reads "Stainless Tri-Mix" and below the screen "CONFIRM" with the Main Control Knob arrow symbol. Data: For MIG Stainless Steel — work/ground clamp cable goes to the NEGATIVE (–) socket, gun/torch cable goes to the POSITIVE (+) socket; shielding gas = Stainless Tri-Mix. Press the Main Control Knob to CONFIRM.

   b. Set Wire Diameter and Material Thickness:
   - Turn Left Knob to set wire diameter.
   - Turn Right Knob to set material thickness.

   > **[Wire Diameter / Material Thickness screen — diagram]** LCD screen titled "MIG Stainless Steel" with a MIG process icon. Two boxed fields: left field headed "⌀ Diameter" showing **.025"**, right field headed "Thickness" showing **24Ga**. Small up/down knob indicator icons flank the fields (left knob icon at far left, right knob icon at far right). Bottom of screen reads "CONFIRM" with the Main Control Knob arrow symbol. Data: Left Knob adjusts wire diameter (.025" shown); Right Knob adjusts material thickness (24Ga shown); press Main Control Knob to confirm.

   c. Auto Weld Settings:
   - Turn Left Knob to adjust Wire Feed Speed (Amperage).
   - Turn Right Knob to adjust Voltage.

   > **[Auto Weld Settings screen — annotated diagram]** LCD weld screen with two large value boxes at top: left box shows **121 in/min** with **0 A** beneath it; right box shows **13.8 V** with **0.0 V** beneath it. A middle information band lists: "MIG Steel" with a process icon, "C25" with a gas-bottle icon (labeled by callouts as Process and Gas Type on the left side), and on the right "⌀ .030"" and "24Ga" (labeled by callouts as Wire Diameter and Material Thickness). A bottom status bar shows three items: a symbol with "25%", a symbol with "5", and a clock symbol with "0.0 s", with "SETTINGS" and the Main Control Knob arrow in the center. Callout labels beneath the bottom bar identify these as, left to right: **Run-In WFS** (25%), **Inductance** (5), **Spot Timer** (0.0 s). Data: Left Knob = Wire Feed Speed/amperage (121 in/min, 0 A actual); Right Knob = Voltage (13.8 V set, 0.0 V actual); displayed process MIG Steel, gas C25, wire .030", thickness 24Ga; Run-In WFS 25%, Inductance 5, Spot Timer 0.0 s accessed via SETTINGS (Main Control Knob).

**Note:** If the Wire Feed Speed or Voltage settings are adjusted manually, the white mark on the line shows the recommended setting for your wire/electrode diameter and workpiece thickness.
