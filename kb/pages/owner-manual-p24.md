# TIG/Stick Welding

> **[Safety alert icon — symbol]** Exclamation point inside a triangle preceding the safety-read notice.

Read the **<u>ENTIRE</u> IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION** section at the beginning of this manual including all text under subheadings therein before set up or use of this product.

> **WARNING** — TO PREVENT SERIOUS INJURY FROM ACCIDENTAL OPERATION:
> Turn the Power Switch off and unplug the Welder before setup.

**Note:** Remove the protective foam and cardboard from the Welder before setup. Place the Welder on a level surface that can bear its weight near the work area. Leave space around the Welder for proper air flow.

## TIG Setup

### Connect Cables

> **[TIG cable connection diagram — diagram]** Three-quarter view of the OmniPro 220 welder with TIG accessories connected at the front panel, with leader-line callouts. Data: Callouts, clockwise — "Foot Pedal Socket (inside Welder)" indicated by a dashed leader pointing into the interior behind the front panel opening; "Foot Pedal (sold separately)" pointing to the pedal unit at left; "Foot Pedal Cable" pointing to the cable running from the pedal into the hole on the welder front; "TIG Torch Cable in Negative Socket" pointing to the cable plugged into the front-panel NEGATIVE (–) socket on the right side; "Ground Clamp Cable in Positive Socket" pointing to the cable plugged into the front-panel POSITIVE (+) socket; "Shielding Gas Hose" pointing to the hose running from the TIG torch assembly toward the rear/gas supply (free threaded fitting shown at left end); "TIG Torch (sold separately)" pointing to the angled-head torch at lower left; "Ground Clamp" pointing to the clamp at bottom center. The welder is drawn with carry handle on top, front control panel with display and rotary knobs, and side ventilation louvers. Polarity fact: for TIG, torch = negative (DCEN), ground clamp = positive.

1. Plug Ground Clamp Cable into Positive Socket. Twist clockwise all the way to lock in place.

2. Plug TIG Torch Cable (TIG Torch sold separately) into Negative Socket. Twist clockwise all the way to lock in place.

3. Insert the Foot Pedal Cable (Foot Pedal sold separately) through hole on Welder front and connect to the Foot Pedal Socket inside the machine. Secure by turning collar clockwise until tight.

4. For connecting Shielding Gas Hose, refer to *Connect Shielding Gas* section on next page.
