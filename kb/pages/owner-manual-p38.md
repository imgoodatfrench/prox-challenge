# Stick Weld Diagnosis–Workpiece Heat Control / Weld Penetration

| INADEQUATE PENETRATION | PROPER PENETRATION | EXCESS PENETRATION OR BURN-THROUGH |
| --- | --- | --- |

> **[Penetration cross-section series — diagram]** Three cross-sectional views of a butt/groove weld through a plate, shown left to right. LEFT ("INADEQUATE PENETRATION"): a small, thin lens-shaped bead sits only at the top surface of the joint; the joint gap below remains an unfilled narrow line, showing the weld did not reach through the material. CENTER ("PROPER PENETRATION"): a large, tapered bead fills the joint and extends fully through the plate thickness, flush/slightly crowned at the top and reaching the bottom surface. RIGHT ("EXCESS PENETRATION OR BURN-THROUGH"): the bead is undercut/hollowed at the top with a concave crater, and the molten metal has blown through the bottom of the plate, bulging below the underside. Data: penetration depth increases from left to right with increasing heat input.

> **[Heat scale arrow — chart]** A wide double-headed black arrow spanning beneath the three cross-sections, mapping each cross-section to a heat condition. Data: left end = "Not hot enough" (under the inadequate-penetration view); center = "Ideal heat" (under the proper-penetration view); right end = "Too hot" (under the excess-penetration/burn-through view).

**Not hot enough** ← → **Ideal heat** ← → **Too hot**

| How to increase workpiece heat and increase penetration:<br>(to weld **THICKER** workpieces properly) | How to reduce workpiece heat and limit penetration:<br>(to weld **THINNER** workpieces properly) |
| --- | --- |
| a. Increase current.  b. Weld more slowly. | c. Decrease current.  d. Weld more quickly. |

# Example Stick Weld Diagrams

**CLEAN WELDS FIRST!**

**Stick welds will have a coat of slag over them until cleaned.**

> **[Example stick weld panels — photo/diagram series]** Seven side-by-side vertical panels separated by double rules. Each panel has (top) a cross-section drawing of the bead profile on a plate, (middle) a close-up black-and-white photograph of the finished bead surface, (bottom) the label, fault name and "TO CORRECT" instruction; the last four panels also include an electrode-angle/arc-length sketch. Data by panel, left to right: (1) **Good Weld** — cross-section shows a medium, evenly rounded bead well fused and slightly spread into the plate; photo shows a uniform, consistent, evenly rippled bead of constant width. (2) **Current Too Low** — cross-section shows a tall, narrow, high-crowned bead sitting up on the surface with little fusion/spread; photo shows a narrow, ropey bead with poor tie-in at the edges. TO CORRECT: Increase the current. (3) **Current Too High** — cross-section shows a wide, flat, low bead spread out over the plate; photo shows a wide, rough, irregular bead with spatter and an eaten-away, undercut appearance. TO CORRECT: Decrease the current. (4) **Weld Speed Too Fast** — cross-section shows a small, narrow, high bead; photo shows a thin bead with elongated, stretched/pointed ripples; the sketch shows the electrode tilted low and moving rapidly to the right (double arrows) leaving a thin deposit. TO CORRECT: Weld slower. (5) **Weld Speed Too Slow** — cross-section shows a wide, thick, piled-up bead; photo shows an excessively wide bead with heavy buildup and rounded, bunched ripples; the sketch shows the electrode more upright with a single short arrow, moving slowly over a heavy deposit. TO CORRECT: Weld faster. (6) **Arc Length Too Short** — cross-section shows a small, low bead; photo shows an uneven bead with coarse ripples and evidence of the rod sticking/stubbing. The sketch shows the electrode held very close to (nearly touching) the workpiece with an upward arrow indicating the electrode should be raised. TO CORRECT: Increase distance. (7) **Arc Length Too Long** — cross-section shows a small, low bead; photo shows a very irregular, ragged, heavily spattered bead with poor fusion at the edges. The sketch shows the electrode held far above the workpiece with a downward arrow indicating the electrode should be lowered. TO CORRECT: Decrease distance.

| Panel | Condition | TO CORRECT |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Good Weld | — |
| 2 | Current Too Low | Increase the current |
| 3 | Current Too High | Decrease the current |
| 4 | Weld Speed Too Fast | Weld slower |
| 5 | Weld Speed Too Slow | Weld faster |
| 6 | Arc Length Too Short | Increase distance |
| 7 | Arc Length Too Long | Decrease distance |
