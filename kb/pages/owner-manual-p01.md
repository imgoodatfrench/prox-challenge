# Owner's Manual & Safety Instructions

**Save This Manual** Keep this manual for the safety warnings and precautions, assembly, operating, inspection, maintenance and cleaning procedures. Write the product's Date Code in the back of the manual (or month and year of purchase if product has no number). Keep this manual and the receipt in a safe and dry place for future reference.

## MULTIPROCESS WELDING SYSTEM

> **[Cover product photo — photo]** Three-quarter front view of the Vulcan OmniPro 220 multiprocess welder: a gray boxy inverter cabinet inside a black wraparound roll-cage frame with a large black carry handle across the top. Data: Front control panel (upper left of unit) bears a small "VULCAN" logo and an "OMNIPRO 220" badge at upper right; a rectangular LCD display shows the text "MIG Steel C25" with a weld-puddle icon and a row of small icons/segments along the bottom of the screen. A knob is at the far left of the display labeled "MENU"(illegible small text) and a knob at the far right labeled "BACK". Below the display are three rotary control knobs in a row, flanked by a small knob at each side. Below the knobs is a black panel with the "VULCAN" chevron logo. Lower front has a rocker power switch on the left and a louvered vent panel. Across the bottom front are three round output/connector sockets (Dinse-style terminals) plus a smaller opening. The right side of the machine is a gray louvered/vented cabinet with an "OMNIPRO 220" badge and a large "VULCAN" chevron logo.
