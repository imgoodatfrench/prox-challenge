# Interior Controls

> **[Interior Controls — labeled diagram]** Line-art side view of the Vulcan OmniPro 220 with the side access door removed, showing the interior wire-feed compartment. The machine is drawn from the spool-side, with the carry handle across the top and the two curved roll-cage/handle tubes at left and right. Callout leader lines identify nine interior components. Data: Callouts, arranged clockwise from the top center — **Cold Wire Feed Switch**: small rectangular rocker/button switch mounted on the upper interior panel, just left of the right-hand upright, above and behind the wire feed mechanism. **Feed Tensioner**: the knurled/finned adjustment knob on top of the wire drive assembly at the upper right of the feed mechanism (points down to the tensioner on top of the drive unit). **Idler Arm**: the pivoting arm on the right side of the wire drive assembly that carries the pressure roller and is clamped down by the feed tensioner. **Feed Roller Knob**: the round knob at the lower right/front of the wire drive assembly that retains the drive feed roller. **Wire Feed Mechanism**: the complete drive-roll assembly, labeled VULCAN on its body, mounted on the right interior wall of the compartment. **Wire Inlet Liner**: the guide tube entering the left/inlet side of the wire feed mechanism, where wire from the spool enters the drive rolls (leader points to the inlet between the spool and the drive assembly). **Wire Spool**: the large circular spool mounted on the hub at the center-left of the compartment. **Spool Knob**: the lobed/star-shaped retaining knob at the center of the wire spool hub that holds the spool on the spindle. Along the bottom of the compartment floor/lower panel are two round multi-pin connectors — **Foot Pedal Socket** (left socket, multi-pin round connector) and **Wire Feed Control Socket** (right socket, multi-pin round connector).
