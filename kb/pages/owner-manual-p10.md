# MIG/Flux-Cored Wire Welding

> ⚠️ Read the **<u>ENTIRE</u> IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION** section at the beginning of this manual including all text under subheadings therein before set up or use of this product.

**WARNING** — TO PREVENT SERIOUS INJURY FROM ACCIDENTAL OPERATION:
Turn the Power Switch off and unplug the Welder before setup.

**Note:** Remove the protective foam and cardboard from the Welder before setup.

## Wire Spool Installation/Wire Setup

1. **Turn the Power Switch OFF and unplug the Welder before proceeding.**

> **[Figure 1 — diagram] Power Switch OFF and unplug.** Close-up line art of the welder's front/side panel showing the rocker Power Switch located below the VULCAN logo and above the two weld-cable Dinse sockets, with a leader line labeled "Power Switch". To the right, a three-prong grounded power cord plug is shown with a curved arrow pointing away from a wall receptacle, indicating the plug should be pulled out of the outlet. Data: Power Switch is on the panel face below the VULCAN branding; cord is a 3-prong (grounded) plug removed from a standard wall outlet.

2. Pull up on the Door Latch, then open the Door.

> **[Figure 2 — diagram] Welder side view with Door and Door Latch.** Line drawing of the welder viewed from the side, with carry handle on top and roll-cage/handle posts at each side. A leader labeled "Door" points to the large rectangular side access panel; a leader labeled "Door Latch" points to the small latch located at the lower center-right of the door. Data: The wire compartment Door occupies the side panel; the Door Latch is a small vertical latch near the bottom center of that door and is pulled UP to release.

3. **<u>1-2 Pound</u> Wire Spool Installation:** Remove the Wingnut and Spacer. If replacing a Spool, remove the old Spool and all remaining wire from the liners.

4. Place the new Wire Spool over the Spool Spindle and against the Spool Brake Pad as illustrated. **To prevent wire feed problems, set the Spool so that it will unwind <u>clockwise</u>.**

5. Replace the Spacer over the Spool Spindle and secure Spool in place with the Wingnut.

**Notice:** If Wire Spool can spin freely, Wingnut is too loose. This will cause the welding wire to unravel and unspool which can cause tangling and feeding problems.

> **[Figure 3 — diagram] 1-2 lb Spool Loading (exploded assembly order).** Exploded view showing, from top to bottom, the order of parts stacking onto the spool spindle: **Wingnut** (top), **Spacer** (below it), **Spool Spindle** (threaded stud, below that), then the **1-2 lb Wire Spool** (drawn as a wound wire spool with upper and lower flanges) seated on the **Spool Brake Pad**, which sits against the **Welder Wall** (the vertical/base wall plate at the bottom). Caption beneath: "1-2 lb Spool Loading". Data: Assembly sequence onto spindle = spool first (against brake pad, seated toward welder wall), then spacer, then wingnut on top; spool must be oriented to unwind clockwise.
