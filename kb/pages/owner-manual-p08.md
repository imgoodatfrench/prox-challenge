# Controls

## Front Panel Controls

> **[Front Panel Controls — labeled diagram]** Front view line drawing of the Vulcan OmniPro 220 welder showing the upper control panel (branded "VULCAN" left, "OMNIPRO 220" right) and the lower connection panel, with leader lines to fourteen callouts. Data: Upper control panel — **Home Button** (left-hand rectangular button marked "HOME", with warning/manual icons above it) on the far left of the panel; **LCD Display** in the center of the upper panel (large rectangular screen); **Back Button** (right-hand rectangular button marked "BACK") to the right of the display. Below the display are three rotary knobs, each drawn as a round knob with a downward-pointing triangular pointer: **Left Knob** (leader from the left; adjacent panel icons show a "+/amperage" symbol ⊕/Ⓐ to its left), **Control Knob** (the larger center knob), and **Right Knob** (right knob, with a voltage symbol Ⓥ to its right). Middle of the machine: **Storage Compartment** behind/above the molded "VULCAN" nameplate on the front housing (leader points to the right side of the compartment cover); **MIG Gun/Spool Gun Cable Socket** is the large round threaded socket at the left of the recessed lower bay; **Power Switch** is the rocker switch (marked with I/O) in the center of the lower bay, to the right of the gun socket; a louvered vent panel occupies the right of the lower bay. Bottom connection strip, left to right: **Spool Gun Gas Outlet** (round threaded gas fitting at far left, next to a gas-fitting icon), **Negative Socket** (Dinse-style twist socket marked with a "–" sign), **Wire Feed Power Cable** (small round port/grommet between the negative and positive sockets), and **Positive Socket** (Dinse-style twist socket at far right marked with a "+" sign).

**Note:** When using an optional Spool Gun (sold separately), connect the Spool Gun gas hose to the Spool Gun Gas Outlet.
